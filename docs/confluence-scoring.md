# Confluence scoring

## Archival definition

The **confluence surface** is what **HOSTAGE STATUS** **puts in one frame**: **GEX Regime**, **GEX Confidence**, **Score (NEG/POS)**, and **Net / Mode**, read together with **SPY/QQQ** tile lines for **opening range structure** and **VWAP relationship**. Those cells compress **A-side** checkpoint selections and **C-side** inputs (manual toggles plus **composite ORB** validity) under **Regime Mode** (MANUAL, AUTO, HYBRID) into **NEG** / **POS** / **NEUTRAL** labeling and confidence copy.

The aim is **interpretive**: to make **agreement and tension among the chart’s own fields** legible at a glance—not to instruct action.

## Not a playbook

**Score (NEG/POS)** and **Net / Mode** report the **netted A-side vs C-side read** the table shows for the bar at hand. That read **depends on** which toggles and checkpoints are set, how **SPY/QQQ** **ORB** summaries combine, which **Regime Mode** is active, and **regime context** (including **NF1** flattening the auto branch).

**Allowed Plays**, **Flip Risk**, **0DTE Gate**, **Execution Mode**, and **Rule** are **rows on the artifact**—name them as **preserved surface text**, not as guidance.

## ORB and VWAP in the same frame

**Opening range structure** is carried in tile **ORB** strings; **VWAP relationship** sits beside them as **Above** / **Below** / **STR**. **GEX regime** headlines both table and tiles. The **confluence surface** is where those strands **appear together**.

## Regime context

The same **GEX Regime** label **reads differently** when ORB phase, tile visibility, or **Regime Mode** branch changes. Outputs are **historical readings fixed in the saved dashboard**, not claims that hold for every later session.

## Scope

Descriptive and archival: how the artifact **represents** confluence—**not** a guide to anything off the chart.

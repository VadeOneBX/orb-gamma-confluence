# Confluence scoring

## Definition (archival)

In this research, **confluence** names a **composed view** in which several independent readings are mapped into a **common scoring or state space** so they can be compared side by side. The intent is **epistemic**, not prescriptive: to make overlap and tension **visible**, not to issue instructions.

Confluence constructs are **research surfaces**. They answer questions of the form: *Under this definition of agreement, how often did these streams line up, and in what shapes?* They do **not** answer: *What should be done next in a live account?*

## Not a signal recipe

Scoring rules documented here—or implied by the dashboard—should be understood as **definitions chosen for inquiry**, not as validated alpha. A confluence score is **conditional** on:

- Which inputs were admitted
- How they were normalized or scaled
- Which time alignment and session assumptions were used
- Which constraint geometry was active

Changing any of those axes changes the meaning of the score. That variability is **by design**: it marks the construct as **hypothesis-bearing**, not as a finished indicator.

## Relationship to opening range and gamma

Opening-range structure supplies **temporal and positional context**: where price has traded relative to a defined opening window. Gamma-related readings supply **a distinct conceptual axis**—often about positioning or convexity-style pressure in the narrative of the work. Confluence scoring is the **tertiary layer** that asks whether those axes **cohere** under a chosen rule set.

None of these layers is privileged as “truth.” They are **orthogonal lenses** brought into a single layout for **comparative study**.

## Regime sensitivity

Confluence summaries **inherit** regime dependence from their inputs. When liquidity, volatility, or session structure shifts, the **same numerical score** may describe a different economic story. The dashboard preserves **scores as artifacts of their regime assumptions**; it does not assert regime invariance.

## What this document does not do

This text does not specify vendor fields, wire formats, execution hooks, or automation. It stays at the level of **what confluence meant in the research** and **how it should be read** in a historical, non-operational frame.

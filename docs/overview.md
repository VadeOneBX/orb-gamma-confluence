# Overview

## Artifact status

This project is archived as a **historical research artifact**. It captures a period of structured inquiry organized around a **dashboard**: a single, persistent visual object that holds together multiple conceptual streams—opening-range context, gamma-oriented readings, and composed “confluence” summaries—so they can be compared under a common layout.

The dashboard is the **lasting output** of that effort. Code, notebooks, or transient experiments may exist at the margins of the tree; the narrative here treats them as supporting evidence, not as a product surface. The authoritative story is the **documented design** and the **dashboard’s role** as a research instrument.

## Research surface, not instruction

Nothing in this documentation should be read as a directive to trade, route orders, or operate infrastructure. Confluence-style summaries are **organizing devices for study**: they compress many signals into a legible form so a researcher can ask whether, in a given historical window, those signals **aligned** in ways worth naming. They are not claims that the same alignment will repeat or that any particular action follows.

## Constraints and regime

**Constraints** in this work are **design variables**: thresholds, filters, and gating rules chosen to shape what the dashboard emphasizes. They define an **exploratory geometry**, not a validated edge or a guarantee about future market behavior.

**Regime** is used in the sense of **interpretive environment**: liquidity conditions, volatility state, session structure, and data availability that color how the same numerical readings should be read. Regime labels or partitions are **context-dependent**; they do not substitute for judgment about the present environment.

## Explicit exclusions

The preserved narrative does **not** cover:

- Data vendor setup or credentials
- Order execution, routing, or operational workflows
- Broker or exchange connectivity
- Schedulers, bots, or automation frameworks
- Hosting, scaling, or deployment playbooks

Those boundaries keep the artifact focused on **architecture of thought and display**, not on operating a live system.

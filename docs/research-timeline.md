# Research timeline

## Chronology as narrative

This timeline orders the work as a **sequence of research moves**, not as a release calendar. Dates are less important than **dependencies**: which questions had to be settled before the dashboard could stabilize, and which refinements followed once the surface was legible.

## Phase framing (historical)

The following phases are **descriptive** of the preserved arc. They are not a methodology mandate for future projects.

1. **Problem framing**  
   The thread began by naming the **comparative question**: how opening-range structure and gamma-oriented readings might be **viewed together** without collapsing one into the other. The dashboard was proposed as the **shared frame** for that comparison.

2. **Surface construction**  
   Attention turned to **layout and legibility**: what must appear on one screen, what could be deferred, and how to avoid false precision. The dashboard took shape as the **primary object**; scoring ideas were subordinate to **readability**.

3. **Confluence articulation**  
   With the surface in place, confluence-style summaries were introduced as **compression layers**—ways to state agreement or tension across inputs. This phase treated scoring as **hypothesis**: definitions to be argued with, not finalized signals.

4. **Constraint exploration**  
   Constraints were adjusted to reveal **structure in the historical record** under different emphases. The outcome was a **documented parameter posture** tied to the preserved dashboard, not a claim of universal optimality.

5. **Regime-aware reading**  
   Later passes stressed **interpretive context**: the same composites read differently under different environment assumptions. Regime language was integrated as **annotation and caution**, not as a separate automated classifier requirement.

6. **Artifact closure**  
   The work concluded by **stabilizing the narrative and the dashboard definition** as historical objects. Further execution, connectivity, or deployment paths were explicitly **out of scope** for what was preserved.

## How to use this timeline

Use it to understand **why** the dashboard looks and reads as it does—not as a checklist for rebuilding operational infrastructure. The lasting output remains the **composed research surface** and the **documentation** that explains how to read it **archivally**.

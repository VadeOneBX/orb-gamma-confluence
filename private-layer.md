# Private layer

The **private layer** names judgment, context, and inference that **do not appear on the dashboard surface**—tacit reading habits, provisional doubts, or narrative that stayed in notes and conversation rather than in the composed view.

This file marks a **boundary**, not a hidden system. Nothing here implies undisclosed mechanics beyond what the shelf narrative describes; the layer is **interpretive residue** that historical research routinely carries outside any fixed display.

Readers should treat the **shelf and dashboard artifacts** as the stable public record of design logic; the private layer is the **acknowledged gap** where individual interpretation remained.
